Why Agility for Shelties?

Shetland Sheepdogs are working dogs with a strong herding instinct built for quick direction changes, controlled stops and problem-solving. A well-built agility course channels that drive into a structured activity that satisfies both the body and the brain. The AKC breed profile notes that Shelties excel in dog sports like agility and rally, where their size and speed give them a natural advantage.

Obstacle Guide

ObstacleWhat It Looks LikeTip for Your Sheltie
TunnelCollapsible fabric tunnel, 6–10 ftStart with a short tunnel at the entry; Shelties are curious and will follow a treat through.
JumpPVC or bar jump, 6–12 in highBegin low for your Sheltie's short legs; use a target stick to guide over.
Weave poles6 poles in a line, 3–4 ft apartWalk slowly between poles at first; Shelties learn the footwork quickly.
TireTire or hoop on a standHold the hoop steady and lure through with a treat at nose height.
PlatformFlat square, 2×2 ft, 4 in highTeach a "place" cue on low surfaces; Shelties love having a job to do.
Pause boxMarked square on the groundUse a settle cue in the box — great for impulse control between obstacles.
Weave poleSingle pole, work around itAdd these singly at first, then link into a pattern as your Sheltie gains confidence.

Sample Course Layouts

Beginner (4 obstacles): Tunnel → Jump → Platform → Pause box. Start with wide spacing and no tight turns.

Intermediate (8 obstacles): Jump → Weave poles → Tire → Platform → Tunnel → Pause box → Weave pole → Jump. Add a gentle turn between obstacles.

Advanced (12 obstacles): Fill most of the grid with a serpentine pattern alternating jumps and weave elements, with a tunnel midway and a pause box before the final jump.

How to Print Your Course Map

  1. Build your course on the grid above.
  2. Optionally name your course.
  3. Click "Print map" to open a clean black-and-white version.
  4. Use your browser print (Ctrl+P) to save as PDF or print on paper.
  5. Click "Back to editor" to make changes.

About this tool

Build a custom agility course for your Sheltie by placing obstacles on a 4×5 grid. Each obstacle type targets a different skill: speed, focus, coordination or impulse control. Print the map and take it to your training area.

Agility tips

  • Warm up with a 5-min sniff walk before running the course.
  • Use high-value treats as rewards for each completed obstacle.
  • Run the course at your Sheltie's pace — speed comes with confidence.
  • End on an easy obstacle so the session finishes on a win.
